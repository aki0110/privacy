Privacy Policy

This app respects users' privacy and establishes this Privacy Policy (the “Policy”) to appropriately handle information related to users.

Users are asked to review and agree to this Policy before using this app.

1. Information We Collect

This app may collect the following information to provide, maintain, and improve the service.

1.1 Account Information

  • Email address
  • User ID issued by authentication services such as Firebase Authentication
  • Information related to login methods
  • Nickname, profile image, or avatar image
  • Language settings, notification settings, and other in-app settings

1.2 Posts, Votes, and App Activity

  • Content posted by users, such as question titles, body text, categories, and choices
  • Images attached to posts
  • Voting and answer information, including the selected choice and answer time
  • Activity information such as favorites, reports, and hidden content
  • Information necessary for handling reports, such as report reasons, reported content, and report time

1.3 Purchases and Subscriptions

  • Information related to App Store in-app purchases, such as transaction ID, product ID, purchase date, expiration date, and renewal status
  • Information necessary to confirm and manage premium membership status

Payment information such as credit card numbers is processed by Apple Inc. and is not directly collected by this app.

1.4 Support Inquiries

  • Email address, inquiry details, issue details, and device information entered into the inquiry form
  • Communication details necessary for support responses from this app

1.5 Device, Usage, and Diagnostic Information

  • Information such as device type, OS version, and app version
  • Crash logs, error logs, and performance-related information
  • Verification information necessary for preventing abuse and protecting security
  • Device identifiers and related information necessary for ad delivery
  • IP address and country or region information estimated from the IP address or similar information

2. How We Collect Information

This app collects user information through the following methods.

  • Information entered, posted, or sent by users in the app
  • Information generated when users vote, favorite, report, change settings, or perform other actions
  • Information obtained through external services such as Firebase Authentication, App Store, Google AdMob, and Firebase Crashlytics
  • Information provided by users through inquiry forms, email, or similar methods
  • Information automatically sent from the device or app when using this app

3. Purposes of Use

Collected information is used for the following purposes.

  • To provide login, identity verification, and account management
  • To provide app features such as posting, voting, favorites, reporting, translation, and premium features
  • To display, aggregate, and analyze posted content and voting results
  • To maintain a safe community, including reviewing inappropriate posts, deleting content, restricting visibility, and limiting use
  • To respond to inquiries and send important notices
  • To confirm subscriptions, manage expiration dates, prevent fraudulent purchases, and respond to refunds, cancellations, and related matters
  • To investigate bugs, improve quality, understand usage, and improve features
  • To prevent and investigate abuse, spam, violations of terms, and security issues
  • To display advertisements and manage ad delivery
  • To comply with laws, terms, and requirements of Apple and other platforms
  • To estimate users' country or region and use such information for aggregation, display, analysis, and service improvement related to posts and voting results

4. Visibility of User-Generated Content

In this app, questions, choices, images, voting results, nicknames, avatar images, and similar content posted by users may be displayed to other users within the app. Please do not post information that can identify you or information you do not wish to make public.

Report details and inquiry details are generally not disclosed to other users. However, they may be reviewed and used as necessary for handling reports, investigating abuse, complying with laws, and protecting the rights of users or third parties.

5. Use of External Services

This app may use the following external services to the extent necessary to provide the service. The handling of information by each service is governed by the privacy policy and related terms of each provider.

  • Firebase Authentication (Google LLC): login and authentication
  • Cloud Firestore / Cloud Functions for Firebase / Firebase Storage (Google LLC): data storage, server-side processing, and image storage
  • Firebase App Check (Google LLC): abuse prevention
  • Firebase Crashlytics (Google LLC): crash analysis
  • Google AdMob (Google LLC): ad delivery
  • App Store / StoreKit / App Store Server Notifications (Apple Inc.): in-app purchases and subscription management
  • OpenAI API (OpenAI, L.L.C.): AI analysis and summaries related to posted content and voting results
  • Google Forms (Google LLC): support inquiry reception
  • IPinfo (IDB LLC): estimation of country or region information based on IP address

Posted content, voting results, IP addresses, device information, and similar information may be sent to external services to the extent necessary to provide translation features, AI analysis features, ad delivery features, country or region estimation features, and related services.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
OpenAI Privacy Policy: https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy
Apple Privacy Policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

6. Advertising

This app may use Google AdMob for ad delivery. For ad delivery, Google LLC may collect information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, and usage information.

This app does not provide users' email addresses or in-app user IDs to advertising providers for the purpose of tracking users across other companies' apps or websites. If personalized advertising or features that constitute tracking are introduced in the future, users will be notified or asked for consent as necessary.

7. Third-Party Disclosure and Service Providers

This app does not provide personal information to third parties without user consent, except in the following cases.

  • When required by law
  • When necessary to protect a person's life, body, or property
  • When disclosure is requested by a public authority through a valid procedure
  • When necessary to protect the rights, interests, or safety of users or third parties
  • When information is provided to external service providers or contractors to the extent necessary for providing the service, processing payments, delivering ads, analyzing crashes, responding to inquiries, or similar purposes
  • When information is provided to the extent necessary in connection with business transfer, succession, merger, or similar procedures

When information is handled by external service providers or contractors, this app will have such information handled only to the extent necessary to achieve the purposes of use.

8. Joint Use

At this time, this app does not jointly use collected personal information with other business operators. If joint use is conducted in the future, the app will publicly disclose, in accordance with applicable laws, the items of information to be jointly used, the scope of joint users, purposes of use, the party responsible for management, and other required matters.

9. Information Management and Security

This app implements reasonable security measures to prevent leakage, loss, damage, unauthorized access, and alteration of collected information. It also strives to prevent abuse through mechanisms such as authentication, access control, encrypted communications, and App Check.

10. Retention and Deletion of Information

Collected information is retained for the period necessary to achieve the purposes of use. When a user withdraws or deletes their account, account information will be deleted or anonymized.

However, some information such as posts, answers, reports, and purchase history may be retained for a certain period to the extent necessary for legal compliance, abuse prevention, fee and purchase management, report handling, inquiry handling, maintaining service integrity, and other operational needs.

11. Account Deletion

Users can delete their account or withdraw from the app through the settings screen in the app. When an account is deleted, authentication information necessary for login and in-app account information will be deleted or anonymized.

If a user has an App Store subscription, deleting the account may not automatically cancel the subscription on the App Store. Please cancel subscriptions through the Apple ID subscription management screen.

12. External Links

This app and related pages may contain links to websites or forms operated by external providers. Please review the privacy policies and related terms of each linked destination regarding the handling of personal information. This app is not responsible for the content of linked destinations or their handling of personal information.

13. Use by Minors

This app is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. Minors should use this app only with the consent of a parent or guardian.

14. Requests for Disclosure, Correction, Deletion, or Suspension of Use

Users may request disclosure, correction, deletion, suspension of use, or similar handling of their own information held by this app. To make such a request, please contact us through the contact information provided at the end of this Policy. Depending on the request, identity verification may be required.

Some requests may not be fulfilled in whole or in part due to legal requirements or operational reasons. In such cases, the reason will be explained to the extent possible.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This app may change this Policy in response to changes in laws, service content, external services used, or other circumstances. If there are important changes, notice will be provided in the app or on this page. The revised Privacy Policy will take effect when posted on this page.

16. Contact

For inquiries regarding this Policy, please contact us through the support contact provided in the app or on the App Store.
