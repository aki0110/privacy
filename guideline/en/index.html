Community Guidelines

These guidelines are rules for creating a safe place where everyone using this app can ask questions and vote with peace of mind.

In this app, you can casually post everyday dilemmas, topics you are curious about, or questions you would like to ask others. However, depending on the content, a post may hurt other users, make them feel unsafe, or lead to trouble.

Please follow these guidelines so that all users can use the app safely and comfortably.

1. Basic Policy

This app is a service for learning other users’ opinions through voting. When creating posts or choices, please be considerate of others and do not use the app to hurt, attack, or harass anyone.

  • Respect other people.
  • Do not attack specific individuals or groups.
  • Avoid posts that may cause anxiety, confusion, or misunderstanding.
  • Respect personal information and privacy.
  • Do not treat voting results as overly definitive.

2. When Posting

When posting a question or consultation, please write it clearly and as neutrally as possible so that other users can answer comfortably.

  • Set choices that are easy to answer for the question, and avoid mismatches between the question and its choices.
  • Avoid extremely biased, inflammatory, or misleading wording.
  • Do not include another person’s personal information, face photo, contact details, address, workplace, school name, or similar information.
  • If you use an image in a post, please use only images that you have the right to use.
  • Do not post content that names a specific individual in order to judge, criticize, or defame them.

3. Prohibited Posts and Conduct

Posts and conduct that fall under any of the following are prohibited. If we determine that a post or action falls under these categories, we may delete the post, restrict its display, restrict the account, or take other measures.

3.1 Defamation and Harassment

  • Content that attacks or insults other users, individuals, organizations, companies, or similar parties.
  • Content that harms someone’s appearance, personality, ability, attributes, or similar characteristics.
  • Content that may lead to harassment, threats, stalking, doxxing, or similar behavior.

3.2 Discriminatory or Hostile Expression

  • Discriminatory content based on race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, gender, sexual orientation, gender identity, disability, age, or similar attributes.
  • Content that incites hatred, exclusion, or violence toward people with specific attributes.
  • Content that unfairly disparages a particular region, culture, occupation, position, or similar group.

3.3 Personal Information and Privacy Violations

  • Posting a person’s name, address, phone number, email address, social media account, or similar information without their permission.
  • Posting a face photo or image that can identify a person without their permission.
  • Disclosing a third party’s secrets, private life, workplace, school, relationships, or similar information.

3.4 Sexual or Obscene Content

  • Excessively sexual expression, obscene images, or obscene text.
  • Content intended for sexual encounters, sexual services, prostitution, or similar purposes.
  • Content involving or suggesting sexual exploitation of minors.

3.5 Violence, Self-Harm, and Dangerous Acts

  • Content that encourages or glorifies violence, criminal acts, or self-harm.
  • Content that promotes harm, threats, or dangerous acts toward others.
  • Content that recommends actions that may endanger life or physical safety.

3.6 Illegal or Fraudulent Conduct

  • Content that encourages crime, illegal drugs, fraud, unauthorized access, copyright infringement, or similar conduct.
  • Sales, solicitation, or promotion of products or services that violate laws or regulations.
  • Impersonation, spreading false information, or spam behavior.

3.7 Advertising, Solicitation, and Commercial Nuisance

  • Excessive advertising, promotion, or attempts to direct users to external services.
  • Investment schemes, information products, multi-level marketing, fraudulent solicitation, or similar content.
  • Repeatedly posting the same or substantially similar content.

3.8 Other Inappropriate Content

  • Content that significantly differs from the purpose of this app.
  • Content that interferes with other users’ ability to use the app safely.
  • Any other content that the operation team determines to be inappropriate.

4. When Voting or Answering

Voting is an important feature that helps posters learn what others think. You may answer casually, but voting results only represent responses from users within this app. They do not represent society as a whole or indicate a correct answer.

  • Do not use voting results to attack others or make overly definitive claims.
  • Do not manipulate vote counts by improper means.
  • Do not interfere with the operation of the service by posting or voting in large volumes, repeatedly posting the same content, or similar behavior.

5. Reporting

If you find an inappropriate post, you can report it using the in-app reporting feature. Reported content will be reviewed by the operation team, and we may delete the post, restrict its display, restrict the account, or take other measures when necessary.

Reporting is a feature for notifying us about rule violations or safety concerns. Please avoid repeatedly reporting posts simply because you disagree with an opinion, dislike the content, or are dissatisfied with the result.

6. Actions by the Operation Team

If the operation team determines that a user has violated, or may have violated, the Terms of Service or these Guidelines, we may take the following actions without prior notice to the user.

  • Deleting posts or images.
  • Restricting the display of posts.
  • Temporarily suspending or restricting an account.
  • Conducting additional investigation of malicious behavior.
  • Responding based on laws, regulations, or requests from public authorities.

We may not individually notify users of the result of every report. We may also be unable to disclose the details or standards used in individual decisions.

7. Tips for Safe Use

  • Do not post information that can identify you or another person.
  • When posting a concern or question, avoid including more personal details than necessary.
  • For matters that require specialized judgment, such as medical, legal, or financial topics, please consider consulting a qualified professional.
  • Treat voting results as reference information, and make final decisions at your own responsibility.
  • If you find a post that makes you uncomfortable or concerned, do not engage with it unnecessarily. Please use the reporting feature.

8. Relationship with the Terms of Service

These Guidelines supplement the app’s Terms of Service. Matters not covered by these Guidelines are governed by the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

9. Changes to These Guidelines

The operation team may change these Guidelines as necessary. If we make important changes, we will notify users in the app or on the official page.

10. Contact

For inquiries regarding these Guidelines, please contact us through the support channel provided in the app or on the App Store.
